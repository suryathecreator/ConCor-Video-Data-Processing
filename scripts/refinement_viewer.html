Sampling and filtering stages

Source annotations → structural checks (valid target, aligned masks, empty-context removal, exact deduplication, 10-track ceiling per entity, 64-track ceiling per expression) → text-only Luna repair of entity links → shared visual packets (up to 12 expressions; split groups over 24 tracks) → Luna context selection, caption repair or rejection → local tag/span and bidirectional-link validation → exact-schema Parquet export.

Both model stages use GPT-6 Luna with medium reasoning. The visual stage sees 12 evenly spaced frame indices including endpoints, plus first appearances. Original frames are paired with numbered overlays at up to 540px height and JPEG quality 88. Before-cleanup evidence is shown for your audit only; it is not a second visual model pass.

Taxonomy and parenthetical glosses stay unlinked. Captions cannot introduce unsegmented background entities. Intentional nonexistent targets are accepted by default; reject only for unsegmented caption context, and link only valid segmented context. More than 10 distinct tracks linked to one entity rejects the whole example.

Retained tracks preserve their complete mask sequences. Errors and pending work are distinct from semantic rejection. Sparse evidence and structural validation do not prove semantic correctness. Dataset media retains its upstream terms.
